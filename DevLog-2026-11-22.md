# HEXOR DEV LOG — Nov 22, 2026

## German & Japanese (v1.0.31)
The console is now fully available in English, 简体中文, 한국어, Deutsch and 日本語 — every page, button, status message, dialog and update flow. Switching language re-renders the whole console instantly. The game-language preference stays separate and is saved with your settings.

## New console look
Fresh design across the app: new sidebar with vector icons and an active-tab indicator, smooth page transitions, cleaner primary/secondary/danger buttons, modern checkboxes, radio buttons and a slim progress bar, plus a pinned status bar. The new HEXOR icon and sidebar logo are integrated everywhere, including the taskbar and desktop icon.

## Smaller, faster updates
- Update packages are now split: most consoles download a console-only package (~60 MB) instead of the full ~119 MB. Older installations automatically use the complete package until their update engine is current — that happened with this release.
- Download time limit raised from 15 to 60 minutes so slower connections can always finish.
- Interrupted a download earlier? The next attempt reuses it if it matches the signed release, instead of starting over.
- Update popups now open centered on your screen.

## The update crash — what happened, fixed & verified
Some of you hit "Hexor update failed: Package checksum is invalid" after yesterday's release. Here's the breakdown:

- The update engine (the small component that installs packages) double-checked the downloaded package against the wrong checksum — it compared the light package against the full package's checksum, so every 1.0.29 console failed the install, every retry.
- Fixed in v1.0.31. Consoles on 1.0.29/1.0.30 automatically take the full package once, which gets them the fixed engine; smaller light packages unlock from there on.
- Verified end to end on a real installation before pushing: the console downloaded, verified, installed and relaunched itself — exactly the flow you'll see.
- Recovery backups were retained the whole time, so nobody needed a manual repair — just reopen Hexor or wait for the next automatic check.
