# Hexor Control Center 1.0.21

## Improvements

- **Free automatic recognition first.** Challenges are now answered by the shared AION recognition service when your updater session is signed in — no key and no per-answer cost. If the shared service is unavailable (signed out, busy, or updated away), Hexor automatically falls back to its own recognition key, so solving never stalls.
- The console reports which service answered each challenge in the status line.
