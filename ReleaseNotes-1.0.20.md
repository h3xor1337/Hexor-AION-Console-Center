# Hexor Control Center 1.0.20

## Fixes

- **Automatic purchases now stay off.** On computers where the older console was never opened, some purchase options did not exist in Windows' settings storage. The game then used its own default — which kept buying items with Abyss, Nightmare and Festival coins even though every switch in the console showed disabled. Hexor now creates every supported option the first time it runs, defaulting purchases to off, and keeps re-asserting your choices every minute.
- Dungeon mode labels now match the game's current wording: "Smart" and "Priority order".

## Improvements

- The update dialog got a cleaner layout: a softer card with a shadow, better spacing and a clearer What's-new list.
