# What changed

## CAPTCHA alerts, simplified
- The old visual detection and pattern templates are gone.
- Hexor now plays a sound the moment any of your game clients shows a challenge.
- Nothing gets paused and nothing asks for confirmation: the auto-resolve just answers.

## Cleaner update dialogs
- Update notes are now easy to read at a glance.

## Fixes from live testing
- Steam installs no longer show up twice.
- Menu status reports correctly on every machine.
