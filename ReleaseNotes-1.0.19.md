# What changed

## Shop purchases fix
- A shop setting that was switched off can no longer be silently re-enabled by the game's own menu: Hexor now re-checks every saved shop setting each minute and restores your choices on the spot.
- Turning a shop option off in Hexor now always sticks.

## CAPTCHA alerts, simplified
- The old visual detection and pattern templates are gone.
- Hexor plays a sound the moment any of your game clients shows a challenge.
- Nothing gets paused and nothing asks for confirmation: the auto-resolve just answers.

## Cleaner update dialogs
- Update notes are now easy to read at a glance.
